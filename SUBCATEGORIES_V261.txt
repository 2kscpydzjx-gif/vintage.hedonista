V261 — catalog subcategory alignment + product editor save behavior

- Nested subcategories now align directly under the parent category instead of being indented.
- Subcategory labels are slightly larger for readability.
- Saving a new or existing product keeps the admin on that product editor page.
- After save, the form is refreshed from Supabase so the administrator can immediately verify the saved values and continue editing.

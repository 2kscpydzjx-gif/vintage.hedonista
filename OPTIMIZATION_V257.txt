Vintage Hedonista V257 — Mobile Performance pass

1. Homepage site_settings request starts from <head> via native fetch, before Supabase SDK/site.js.
2. The dynamic hero main image is preloaded immediately when that early response returns.

4. Below-fold dynamically rendered images receive fetchpriority=low in addition to lazy loading.
5. Google Fonts stylesheet loads non-render-blocking with a noscript fallback.
6. Header/footer logo is a 320px WebP (~12 KB instead of ~86 KB PNG).
7. Cache-busting updated to v257.
